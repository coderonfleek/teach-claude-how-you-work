Hi Maya,

Good piece. The image of one planner AI handing out jobs to the research, analysis and report helpers "like me assigning chores on a Sunday night" is perfect, and the diagram makes the whole thing click.

Two notes:

1. The headline over-promises. "I Let One AI Plan My Week and Got Back 10 Hours" is a big claim, and the piece doesn't back it up. Your own numbers say it saved about 2.5 hours across four Mondays, and one of those Mondays was no faster at all. Readers will feel cheated by paragraph six. The honest version, "I Let One AI Plan My Week", is plenty interesting on its own. If you want a number in there, use the real one. Can you add "check the headline against the body" to your edit checklist?

2. The ending. "Stop managing your week. Let it manage itself." is a slogan, and it's not even what you found. The piece actually ends better two paragraphs earlier, with the suggestion to let an AI split just one Monday task into steps. Make that the "Try this week" and stop there.

The "What didn't work" section, where the research helper merged two of your voice memos into an idea you never had, is the most useful thing in the article. Readers need to hear that.

Send the revised headline when you have it.
