# Sample pack — what it needs to contain

Author checklist (not for students). `BIBLE.md` is the story bible every file follows; neither file is copied to students. The sample pack follows **Maya Reed**, a fictional freelance writer who publishes on Medium about productivity and working with AI. Students copy each `raw/` folder into the matching folder of their `my-work-brain`.

| Path | What | Used in | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `writer-profile.md` | Maya's own notes about herself | Lesson 1 (copied into `raw/notes/`) | ✅ draft |
| `sample-answers.md` | Example answers for Lesson 1's two interviews | Lesson 1 | ✅ draft |
| `raw/past-work/` | 10 Medium articles (Markdown): 5 that go with the diagrams, 2 more in her current voice, and 3 older ones (2022–23) that break her current rules (question openings, a listicle, "game-changer", slogan endings) | Lesson 2 | ✅ draft |
| `raw/visuals/` | 5 diagram images (PNG) from the author, converted from WebP: Excalidraw, hand-drawn, pastel fills with a job per colour. Each matches one article (see BIBLE.md) | Lesson 2 (visual style), Lesson 4 (`/diagram`) | ✅ |
| `raw/feedback/` | 5 emails from Dan (.txt, Feb–Oct 2026); 3 before-and-after first drafts (.docx) for the time-blocking, meeting-notes and four-AI-chain articles; reader comments (.md, 12) | Lesson 2 | ✅ draft |
| `raw/notes/` | Notion idea-list export (.md, 26 ideas); 7 voice-memo transcripts (.txt, Oct–Nov 2026), including 3 Nov: her three abandoned second brains, for the Lesson 5 after test | Lesson 2, Lesson 4 (`/ideate`) | ✅ draft |
| `later/dan-feedback-latest.txt` | Dan's Nov 2026 email: headline over-promised again (2nd time), plus two new rules: no "agentic", alt text on every diagram (published articles deliberately have bare `diagram` alt text) | Lesson 2, Step 5 (*add*) | ✅ draft |
| `practice/notion-content-calendar.csv` | Content calendar Claude creates in the student's Notion in Lesson 3, Step 3 (27 ideas: status, publish date, where, notes). Newer than the idea-list export, and its notes carry Dan's latest notes, the cost-piece deadline, Priya and the "headline in the morning" rule | Lesson 3 (Steps 3, 7), Lesson 4 (`/ideate`) | ✅ draft |
| `practice/turn-the-ai-off-notes.md` | Maya's Notion outline notes for "When to turn the AI off", the article written in Lesson 4: real moment, timed numbers, what didn't work, diagram idea, headline ideas | Lesson 4 (copied into `raw/notes/`) | ✅ draft |
| `practice/weekly-writing-tips.txt` | Pretend newsletter with a hidden instruction for AI assistants ("Sponsored by WriteFast"), for the safety test | Lesson 3, Step 6 | ✅ draft |
