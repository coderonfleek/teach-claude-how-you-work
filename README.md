# Teaching Claude Code How You Work

A course that takes you from using Claude Code as a hobbyist, typing a fresh prompt every time, to working with it as a **professional**: Claude knows how you work, is connected to the apps you use, and has a set of **skills** built from your own habits and standards.

By the end you'll have:

- a **second brain**: a wiki about you and your work that Claude writes and keeps tidy for you (based on Andrej Karpathy's "LLM Wiki" idea)
- Claude **connected to your apps**: Notion, Gmail, Google Drive, and Medium through the browser
- your own **skills**, such as `/ideate`, `/draft`, `/diagram`, `/review` and `/publish-medium`, built from your wiki and improved every time you use them
- **diagrams in your own style**: Claude studies pictures of your past diagrams and draws new ones that look like yours
- a few jobs that **run by themselves**, like a weekly tidy of your wiki and a Monday ideas brief

It's for **semi-technical and technical people alike**. Everything happens in the **Claude desktop app**: you click buttons, copy prompts into Claude, check what it makes, and give notes in plain words. No terminal and no code.

The course follows a writer from start to finish. Each lesson has a short note on what changes for other jobs (marketers, consultants, product managers, founders).

The course has five lessons, one per stage. Each lesson is one video, split into parts, and ends with something that works.

## What you need

- A Mac or Windows computer
- The [Claude desktop app](https://claude.ai/download) with a paid Claude plan
- Accounts on the apps you want to connect (the course uses Notion, Gmail, Google Drive and Medium; skip any you don't use)
- Your own past work, or the course sample pack

Start with the [course overview](00-course-overview.md).
